66books

02 / 07

66 books for a statistical mind: 24 novels where mathematics drives the plot, 24 nonfiction books that build the toolkit for reasoning under uncertainty, and 18 science books on physics, the cosmos and how we know what we know.

  • 66 books
  • 24 fiction + 24 nonfiction + 18 science
  • Railway-themed, with a live departures board
Open page → DetailsHide

What you can do

  • Walk three paths. A five-level statistical path of nonfiction, a nine-novel narrative path and a four-level science path, all meeting at one question. Tap any stage to filter the catalog to those books.
  • Search, filter and sort. Filter by Fiction, Nonfiction, Science or topic, and by status: Finished, Unread, Currently reading or Bought. One button sorts the books on screen from hardest to easiest, another puts them in a recommended reading order. Both work on whatever you have filtered.
  • Read before you buy. Every book has a spoiler-free description, a star rating and four scores out of 10: how intellectual, complicated, thinking-heavy and mind-bending it is. Collapsible details show publisher, length and series, and each book links to Amazon.in and Goodreads.
  • Mark books finished. Pull the lever and a train rolls in to the final destination. Finished books are stamped in green, and you can rate each one on six criteria with ten stars each. The page works out the overall score, and anything scoring 8 or higher glows. Write a review that sits under the book.
  • Download your list. Export whatever is on screen as a styled reading timetable you can save as a PDF.
  • Keep your place. Finished books, ratings, reviews, your current read and what you've bought are saved to your account when you're signed in, and in your browser otherwise.
  • Start small. Tap Essential Six for a six-book starter shelf, or hit Surprise me.

What's inside

Fiction (24). Novels, plays and tales where a mathematician or statistician is a character, or where mathematical thinking drives the plot: The Oxford Murders, Uncle Petros and Goldbach's Conjecture, The Housekeeper and the Professor, Contact, Permutation City and Arcadia.

Nonfiction (24). The toolkit itself: The Drunkard's Walk, The Signal and the Noise, Thinking, Fast and Slow, The Book of Why, Superforecasting and Jaynes's Probability Theory.

Science (18). The big ideas behind it all: Cosmos, The Selfish Gene, A Brief History of Time, Gödel's Proof, The Emperor's New Mind and Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!.

  • Decision theory
  • Causal inference
  • Game theory
  • Physics & cosmology
  • Philosophy of science
