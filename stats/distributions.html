Interactive Distributions

The Distributions — Building Blocks of All Models

Each distribution includes an interactive graph — move the sliders to see how the shape responds. Skewness, kurtosis, and moment generating functions included. The Theory & Relations tab adds how the distributions connect, exponential families, transformations and order statistics, limit theorems, extreme values, concentration, copulas and multivariate distributions at four levels, plus 18 further distributions.
